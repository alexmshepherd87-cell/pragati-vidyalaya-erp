Do this before the ceremony. Enter all vote counts, then press SAVE ALL VOTES. On the day, use the Reveal Ceremony tab — no typing needed.

HOW TO USE

BEFORE THE CEREMONY (today / tonight)

  1. Go to SETUP VOTES tab (chairman only — keep secret)
  2. Enter vote counts under each candidate's photo
  3. Press SAVE ALL VOTES, then Export votes.json and keep the file in this folder

ON THE DAY (ceremony)

  1. Open page → BEGIN THE CEREMONY (starts Head Boy counting)
  2. Counting animates → freezes on final votes (no winner yet)
  3. Press NEXT → winner announced with photo + confetti
  4. Press NEXT again → next post counting (Head Girl, etc.)
  5. After all posts → FINAL RESULTS scoreboard

SOUND

Stadium crowd sound is built into the page — no audio files or internet needed. The crowd murmurs during counting, builds as votes climb, and erupts with cheering and applause when the winner appears. Just make sure the speaker volume is up.

Works offline. Copy the whole PRAGATI-RESULTS-REVEAL folder to USB (include votes.json). Open with OPEN-REVEAL.bat.
